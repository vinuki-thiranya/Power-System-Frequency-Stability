## Frequency-response modelling

8. P. M. Anderson and M. Mirheydar, "A low-order system frequency response model," *IEEE Trans. Power Syst.*, vol. 5, no. 3, pp. 720-729, Aug. 1990, doi: [10.1109/59.65898](https://doi.org/10.1109/59.65898). *Used for:* the idea of reducing a power system to one equivalent machine and governor. Abstract read; full text is behind a paywall.
9. T. G. Jathunga, A. H. Wijethunga, and J. B. Ekanayake, "Impact of inertia constant on frequency dynamics of a power system," *Ceylon J. Sci.*, vol. 50, no. 1, pp. 3-10, 2021, doi: [10.4038/cjs.v50i1.7841](https://doi.org/10.4038/cjs.v50i1.7841). *Used for:* an earlier single-bus model of the Sri Lankan system with its load-shedding scheme; load damping of 1%.
10. T. Bambaravanage, S. Kumarawadu, and A. Rodrigo, "Comparison of three under-frequency load shedding schemes referring to the power system of Sri Lanka," *Engineer: J. Inst. Eng. Sri Lanka*, vol. 49, no. 1, pp. 41-52, 2016, doi: [10.4038/engineer.v49i1.6917](https://doi.org/10.4038/engineer.v49i1.6917). *Used for:* the load-shedding stages and the 0.85 Hz/s rate-of-change element.
11. C. D. Aluthge, K. T. M. U. Hemapala, and J. R. Lucas, "BESS as a UPS to power systems with high solar penetration," *Front. Energy Res.*, vol. 9, Art. no. 653015, 2021, doi: [10.3389/fenrg.2021.653015](https://doi.org/10.3389/fenrg.2021.653015). *Used for:* a battery study on the Sri Lankan grid with a detailed network model.
12. M. Krpan and I. Kuzle, "Introducing low-order system frequency response modelling of a future power system with high penetration of wind power plants with frequency support capabilities," *IET Renew. Power Gener.*, vol. 12, no. 13, pp. 1453-1461, 2018, doi: [10.1049/iet-rpg.2017.0811](https://doi.org/10.1049/iet-rpg.2017.0811). *Used for:* a recent extension of low-order models to inverter-based plant. Abstract read.
